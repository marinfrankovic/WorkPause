Simple by design

One switch.
Zero guesswork.

WorkPause controls whether your schedule runs. It is not another switch for turning your work apps on and off manually.

WorkPause ON

Your schedule takes over.

Work apps follow your saved daily rules. Turning WorkPause on applies the current scheduled state, too.

Applies immediately. No Save needed.
WorkPause OFF

Your phone stays in control.

WorkPause stops making automatic changes. Work apps stay as they are, and your phone's normal controls and policies still apply.

Your saved schedule stays ready for later.

  3. 3

    Make it your week.

    Choose each day's rule, tap Save schedule, and turn WorkPause ON. Allow exact alarms; unrestricted battery use is recommended.

One-time silent-control grant

Connect your phone and authorize USB debugging first.

adb shell pm grant io.github.marinfrankovic.workpause android.permission.MODIFY_QUIET_MODE

Installing without ADB is possible, but does not grant silent control. The optional accessibility fallback visibly operates Quick Settings, and confirmations may need your input. Android and employer restrictions always apply.

Good questions

Before you
switch on.

Does WorkPause OFF turn my work apps off?

No. It stops WorkPause's automatic changes and leaves the current work-profile state untouched. Your phone's normal controls remain available. Your daily rules are kept for later.

Do I need an existing work profile?

Yes. WorkPause schedules an existing Android work profile; it does not create one. Samsung Secure Folder is not a substitute for a managed work profile.

Can I install without a computer?

Yes. Download the APK on your phone and follow Android's installation prompts. Silent control still needs the separate one-time ADB grant from a computer. See the setup guide for the optional visible fallback and sideloading restrictions.

Can WorkPause bypass the phone or work-profile PIN?

No. Silent control requests pause/resume without operating Quick Settings; it does not unlock the profile. When Android or employer policy requires the PIN, you must enter it before work apps become available. WorkPause implements deferred retries within the active work window, but other real-device locked-resume scenarios remain unverified.

Is it free and open source?

Yes. WorkPause is an independently maintained Apache-2.0 fork of Work Profile Scheduler. The source, release APKs, license, and original attribution are available on GitHub.
